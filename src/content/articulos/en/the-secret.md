With the arrival of summer and the end of the school year, our young people's parties begin.  
When you are a mother or father, you know that sooner or later your daughter will tell you she has her first party.  
![](http://www.adiccionesadolescentes.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/fiesta.png)Regardless of the knot in our stomach that those words give us, we would love to tell our little girl that she is still very young and has her whole life ahead of her to go out, that 30 is a good age to start thinking about such things, but not before.  
Well, the day has come, and our daughter goes out "dressed" in clothes that I would describe, in politically correct terms, as minimalist.  
It is also true that, as a "modern" father, I don't think a burka is the only appropriate outfit for these events, but I would prefer something closer to military overalls with a helmet and dark glasses.  
After a glance at her mother, and in reply some words we never want to understand – "it's what girls her age wear now" – it is then, before saying goodbye, that we give her our final, solemn pieces of advice:  
Be careful who you go with!  
Be careful nobody slips anything into your drink!  
Don't even think about drinking alcohol, let alone trying joints!, etc…

These phrases have the opposite effect to the one we want. It is like when we are driving and the person next to us keeps giving us warnings: "watch the car in front!", "you're going too fast!", "the light's about to turn red!", etc. After a while of constant warnings, all you want is for them to lose their voice, or to throw them out of the moving car.

All these alarms achieve is what is called the self-fulfilling prophecy: these warnings cause so much stress that sooner or later a mistake will be made because of the pressure.  
*The Secret* we recommend to parents is to replace the discourse of fear with these three phrases:

**Darling, I love you!**  
**I trust you!**  
**Have a great time!**

These words will not stop us from staying awake and worried until she comes home, but when they open the door and we see that everything has gone well, and they excitedly tell us they've had the best time ever…

Then it will be time to relax and go to bed, knowing that we have lost part of a little girl but gained a piece of a responsible young woman.  
Let's make the most of it and sleep – until the next party!
