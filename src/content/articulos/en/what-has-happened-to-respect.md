Since time immemorial, respect has gone hand in hand with knowledge. The most respected people have been the elderly, because they treasured the experiences of previous generations.  
With the arrival of the information age, the concept of respect has lost much of its value. Anyone can access knowledge on any subject through the Internet, with opinions for and against.  
Where this “respectful” crisis is seen most clearly is with our adolescents:  
- In recent years, assaults on fathers and mothers by their own children have been rising alarmingly.  
- There is a crisis of respect at school, where unmotivated and bored pupils become disruptive and take centre stage in the classroom.

But this is not a problem of our young people alone:  
- When someone is given an appointment at a set time and half an hour, an hour or several hours later they are still kept waiting without the slightest explanation, that is a lack of respect. It reaches its peak when they are finally seen and the late professional does not even offer a simple apology for the delay.  
- When we take man’s best friend out to relieve itself and, out of cheekiness or disgust, we do not pick up its droppings, that is a lack of respect.  
- When politicians elected by the people forget those most in need in order to rub shoulders with the powerful, aspiring to become one of them. That is a lack of respect.  
- When, in a couple of any age, one partner believes they have the right to control the other, even going so far as to insult or hit them. That is more than a lack of respect: it is a sexist act, a crime.

Personally, I believe that respect is a reciprocal feeling: we must give it first.  
Every day, when I go to work with a new adolescent, I know that the first thing I have to do is earn their respect, because they come in the worst possible conditions:

1. Forced to come (by their parents, Social Services or the courts).

2. Determined not to listen and to leave as soon as possible.

3. Convinced that they will never come back.

If we lose respect, we will go backwards on the evolutionary scale, becoming just another animal for whom strength is the one and only powerful argument.
