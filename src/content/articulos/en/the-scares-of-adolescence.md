The scares OF ADOLESCENCE

-   There are key moments in the life of the adolescent:

BIOLOGICAL

With the hormonal "explosion" the child's life will be heading towards adolescence, where the biggest changes in his/her life will take place:

-   \-Their body will grow until they are 20. In some cases they will almost double their weight and height.

- Psychological/Neurological. - A neuronal maturation begins. It will not end until the age of 25.

- Relational. - It will begin with a distancing from parents and an approach to peers.

EDUCATIONAL

- When they move from primary to secondary education, if there is also a change of school, the situation can be stressful.

TECHNOLOGICAL

- When they are given their first cell phone. In addition to discovering a thousand ways of entertainment, they also encounter the other side of the coin: bullying, sexting, grooming, porn, etc.

FAMILY

- Separation or divorce of parents.

-     For many months they have been putting up with insults, disqualifications and even aggressions, sometimes even becoming themselves "throwing weapons" in the war of their parents.
-   The sum of biological vulnerability, plus some of the other stressors, can be the trigger of a crisis, overwhelming the child who does not know what to do, where to go or who to ask for help.
-   If at that moment there is no one close to them with whom they can share, they may end up seeking solace in addictions (alcohol, joints, technology, etc.), as they are a cheap way of "self-medicating" to escape for a few hours and also a way to socialize with others in the same situation.

That is why it is so important to work from the tribe (Family, Teachers, Health, Police, Civil Guard), because as soon as a suspicion appears, it is necessary to start working to prevent it from going further.

**"TEENAGERS ARE STRONGER THAN WE THINK, BUT MORE VULNERABLE THAN THEY INTEND TO MAKE US BELIEVE."**
