**\- WHEN PROTECTING YOUR CHILDREN MEANS WEAKENING THEM -**

Every week we attend to young teenagers who are brought to us, not for smoking joints or drinking alcohol, but for having behavioural problems in school or for having problems with the law, despite their young age.

When we investigate their family nucleus we see that in many occasions they come from broken families, some of them even toxic ones…this means that they have grown up on the streets with hardly any rules or limits, with no choice but to make a living.

If they are also only children, the bond between mother and child is strengthened, making the mother feel, not only responsible, but also guilty for the behaviour of her child.

In addition, we Spaniards have the problem of not having enough children, not even to replace those who are dying, this makes that the few we have, their family and society protect them even more, as a scarce and precious good.

This overprotection leads a mother to sell the washing machine so that her children, who are of age, do not have to face the frustration "of not having internet at home to play", which is all they do.

Later on, the overprotection leads to paying a child for the consumption of marijuana, which will eventually become cocaine, so that "the supplier or dealer does not beat him up".

**If we as parents do not allow our children to face the frustration that it is not possible to have everything, or that to have things you have to pay the price first, if they do not face reality, we will be raising them for a world that does not exist and it will not be long before they become tyrants for parents and potential criminals for society.**  The next article will be entitled:
