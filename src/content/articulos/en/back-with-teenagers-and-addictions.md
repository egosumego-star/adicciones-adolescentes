For a long time I have been firmly intending to change the website, to make it much simpler and more participatory.![How scary!](http://www.adiccionesadolescentes.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/baby-with-laptop-300x199.jpg)

My fears made me question this project, telling myself over and over again: why do I need to stress myself out again, when I already generate more than enough adrenaline with my work, talks and articles?

But I confess that another fear pushes me forward: that of spending my time while giving almost nothing back to the society that has given me everything. I have a debt I will never be able to repay.

Morally, I have an obligation to fail as many times as necessary to get my grain of sand to whoever can use it:

- Whether it is parents, so they can build themselves a beach of calm.
- Other professionals – from any discipline – who work with teenagers, so they can build stronger bridges to reach young people.
- Or any student who can see the great future potential of these subjects.

Well then, let's get going.
