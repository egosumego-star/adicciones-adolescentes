![](http://www.adiccionesadolescentes.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/Invisible2-e1497216842588.jpg)In India, the lowest caste is that of the untouchables, whom everyone avoids because they believe that whatever they touch becomes impure and toxic. In our country, with a certain superiority complex, we consider this kind of discrimination to be typical of a developing country, but not of ours. What is surprising is when we see that castes exist here too, and I don't mean politicians, but ***people who have neither voice nor vote***. They are beings who only appear in the media when they have done something wrong. Yes, I am talking about the caste of the ***invisible ones***. As their name suggests, everyone knows they are there, yet they are ignored. Some parents say this caste is made up of "aliens" who little by little abducted their loving, obedient children until they turned them into strangers who wander around the house complaining at all hours, no matter how much attention they are given, who are always dissatisfied and blame their parents for everything… The health system hardly knows them, because they are the healthiest human beings, and they are usually only seen in A&E. Where they spend most of their time is at school: when they arrive at the first class of the year, the teacher, holding up the textbook, preaches the first maxim to them: "We have to get through the syllabus of this subject in the teaching weeks of the school year." Second maxim: "Forget the educational foundations you learnt for several years in preschool; here there are no round tables where everyone is encouraged to work together and exchange opinions. As you can see, all the chairs in the classroom face the same way, towards the teacher and the blackboard, so I demand maximum attention and silence. You will only break this rule when you are asked a question, and not before." Third maxim: "You, the invisible ones, are responsible for piling data into your brains until exam time, when the content will be assessed and, if you pass, you may proceed to empty it out to prepare for the next term." There is also the maxim that is kept from them: "You, the invisible ones, will spend many years receiving the knowledge that will allow you a more or less advanced position in the dole queue. In theory, the more degrees and master's you have, the more chance you will have of working in a fast-food chain."

Enough is enough! It is time to make our **ADOLESCENTS** visible, because they are the owners of our future. In short, you could say that our well-being and our pensions will depend on them. If we keep ignoring them, it may be that when they are at the top of the system, they will do the same with those who are not productive (people with disabilities, pensioners and retirees), and turn us into ***invisible ones***...
