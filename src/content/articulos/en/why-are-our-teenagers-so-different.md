With the onset of adolescence – between the ages of 10 and 12 – our young people face a series of drastic changes in their lives and in those of their families:

- **Physically**, in just a few years they can go from a size 35 trainer to a 43.
    - Their hips or chest broaden.
    - Body hair appears and sexual characteristics develop.
    - Growth will finish at around 20.
- **Emotionally**, they will begin to mature, but it will not be until around 25 that they reach stability.
- **Parents** go from being the heroes of their lives to repressive humans who are "always" wrong.
- In childhood everyone **played** together; with the arrival of adolescence they start grouping by gender (boys with boys, girls with girls).
- Along with the hormones come not only spots but also a different way of looking at each other: **Desire**.

Everything said so far has been happening since the beginning of time. What makes them different from all previous generations is:

- **Social Adolescence** appears. Thanks to the economic well-being of families over the last 30 years, they no longer need the physical or financial contribution of the youngest members to survive and keep the family going, as all previous generations were forced to do. This has made it possible for anyone who is interested to continue their education, an opportunity that used to be within reach only of well-off families. They are the best-educated generation in history.
- The **Internet** appears. Relationships and minds are changing. Friends used to be limited to the physical surroundings of the neighbourhood or town; now they can chat, laugh and play with others who may be hundreds or thousands of kilometres away, talking in several languages.
- The foundations of **Knowledge** have also been turned upside down, since before, what was written in a book and repeated by teachers was unquestionable truth. Now it is just one truth among the many others the Internet shows us.
- These changes have been so rapid that no university degree has been able to study these new teenagers in depth.
- For **Healthcare**, this new population group is the great unknown.

All of the above makes boys and girls of this age so wonderfully different from those of previous generations.
